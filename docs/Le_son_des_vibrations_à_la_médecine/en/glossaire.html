Glossary

Sound: From Vibration to Medicine

amplitude — Width of the back and forth of the vibration, determining the volume of the sound.
virtual scalpel — Technique using ultrasound to treat heart disease without surgery.
echo — Sound that bounces off an obstacle and returns to the origin.
ultrasound — Technique using ultrasound to produce internal body images.
frequency — Number of vibrations per second, determining the pitch of the sound.
Iconeus — Start-up created by Mickael Tanter to develop ultra-fast ultrasounds.
cE marking — Label allowing the sale and use of medical devices in Europe.
wave — Regular back and forth of molecules carrying sound information.
aortic narrowing — Heart disease caused by hardening of a heart valve.
sound — Vibrations that pass through the air and make it possible to perceive noises.
stamp — Distinctive character of a sound, linked to its source.
ultrasound — Sound having a frequency greater than 20,000 hertz, not perceived by the human ear.
vibration — Repetitive movement of part of an object, source of sound.
