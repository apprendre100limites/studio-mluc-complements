Sound: From Vibration to Medicine

Sound is a form of energy that propagates in the form of vibrations. These vibrations are transmitted from one molecule to another in a material medium, such as air. Sound needs a medium to propagate, and it travels much faster in water or steel than in air.

Sound definition

Sound is created by vibrations. These vibrations can come from an object that is moving, such as a guitar string or vocal cords.

The vibration propagates in the air, it moves from molecule to molecule, forming a sound wave.

Sound needs a medium to propagate. Without air, water, metal, sound cannot move.

In space, there is no air to carry vibrations, so no one can hear a scream.

Takeaway. Sound requires a medium to propagate.

Sound Speed

Sound travels at a speed of 340 meters per second through the air.

Light travels much faster than sound, eight hundred and eighty thousand times faster.

Sound travels faster in media where molecules are closer together, such as water or steel.

Takeaway. Sound travels faster in environments where molecules are close.

Sound Characteristics

Sound has three main characteristics: frequency, amplitude and timbre.

Frequency is the pitch of the sound. A high frequency gives a high pitched sound, a low frequency gives a low pitched sound.

Amplitude is the strength of sound. It is measured in decibels and indicates the magnitude of the vibrations.

Timbre is the quality of sound. It makes it possible to recognize a musical instrument, for example a piano or a guitar.

Takeaway. The timbre makes it possible to recognize a musical instrument.

What's the point?

Sound has many applications in everyday life and in various professions. It is used for music, communication and to treat diseases. Physicists like Michael Tanter use sound to develop medical technologies, such as ultrafast ultrasound and non-invasive heart treatment techniques.

Conclusion

Understanding sound and its characteristics is important to understand how it works and how it can be used to treat diseases.
