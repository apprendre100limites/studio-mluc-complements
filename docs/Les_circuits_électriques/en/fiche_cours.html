Electrical circuits

When you press a switch, the lamp lights up immediately, even if nothing moves with the naked eye. The video explains what really happens in the wires and why the circuit has to form a loop.

The current loop

To light a lamp, it is necessary to connect its two terminals with those of the battery with two conductor wires.

If the chain is interrupted, e.g. by an open switch, no current flows and the lamp remains off; as soon as the chain closes and forms a loop, the current passes from the battery to the lamp.

To be remembered. The current can only circulate if the wires form a closed loop.
Be careful. Thinking that only one wire is enough between the battery and the lamp.

Schemes

Two-point components, such as the battery, lamp, switch or motor, are called dipoles and have standardized symbols used all over the world.

To make a good diagram, draw the threads in straight lines with straight angles and check that the drawing forms a closed loop.

To be remembered. An electrical diagram uses standard symbols and straight-angled wires to ensure a closed loop.
Be careful. Draw wires without right angles or without checking the buckle closure.

Series and diversion

Serial dipoles follow each other on the same loop; if the switch opens or if a grille lamp, the whole chain turns off because the buckle is cut.

In turn, each lamp has its own branch which forms a loop with the battery; Open the switch from one branch does not affect others, each lamp works independently.

To be remembered. In series the dipoles are interlinked, by-pass each branch operates independently.
Be careful. Confound serial and bypass and believe that a grilled lamp turns off all the others.

Complex circuits

In a complex circuit, serial dipoles form branches that are themselves associated by-pass, like two lamps in series on one branch and one lamp alone on another.

To be remembered. A complex circuit combines serial and bypass parts to create multiple loops.

What's the point?

These concepts allow an electrician to design bypass facilities so that each outlet works separately. A photovoltaic technician uses them to connect solar panels in series or by-pass to obtain the desired voltage and intensity. They are also essential in building, energy, automobile and electronics to understand and secure circuits.

Conclusion

A circuit is a closed loop of conductors, drawn with standard symbols, where the serial dipoles are bound and those in bypass remain independent.
