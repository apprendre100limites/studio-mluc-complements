series

adjective

Definition

Arrangement where the dipoles are placed one after the other on the same loop.

Characteristics

Examples

Non-examples

Characteristics: the dipoles are bound; if one is cut, all is extinguished; form one branch
Examples: two lamps in series on the same branch; a circuit where the switch opens the whole chain
Non-examples: Dipoles in bypass where each branch is independent; two lamps on separate branches
diversion

adjective

Definition

Provision where each dipole has its own independent branch.

Examples

Non-examples

Characteristics: the branches are independent; cutting of one branch does not affect others; used in household outlets

Non-examples: a series circuit where all the lamps turn off together; one thread that does not form a separate branch
