Glossary

Electrical circuits

Buckle — The closed path through the current to transfer energy.
Branch — Part of the circuit where there is only one component.
Drivers — Wires that allow the current to flow between the battery and the lamp.
Current — The electrical energy that circulates in the wires to light the lamp.
Derivation — A connection where each component is on its own branch, making them independent.
Dipole — An electrical component that has two terminals, such as the battery, lamp or switch.
Intensity — A size that is obtained by connecting solar panels.
Switch — An element that cuts or closes the circuit to turn on or off the lamp.
Cell — An electrical power source that supplies the lamp.
Diagram — A graphic representation of a circuit using standardized symbols.
Series — A connection where the components follow on the same loop.
Tension — A size that is obtained by connecting solar panels.
