Pharos 1/4 case: The file

  • Alexandria's lighthouse, built between 297 and 283 B.C., was one of the seven wonders of the ancient world.
  • It measured about 103 metres, the height of a thirty-storey building, with three superimposed sections and a statue at the top.
  • Its cost was 800 talents, each talent weighing 21.4 kg, which represents about 17 tons of money.
  • After 1,600 years of existence, he collapsed on August 8, 830 because of an earthquake that destroyed one third of the city.
  • The researchers rely on four families of indexes: texts, currencies, remnants and numerical models.
  • The main function of the lighthouse was to guide ships at night along a dangerous coast.
  • He had to respond to four constraints: to be visible from a distance, visible at night, to resist the sea and the wind, and to stand for a very long time.
