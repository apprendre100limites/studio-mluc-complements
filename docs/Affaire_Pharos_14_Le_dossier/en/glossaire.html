Glossary

Pharos 1/4 case: The file

Cealex — Research unit of the National Centre for Scientific Research in Alexandria.
constraint — a requirement which the lighthouse must comply with, such as being seen from a distance or resisting the wind.
main function — guide night ships along a dangerous coast.
Digital twin — virtual representation of the lighthouse used for study and simulation.
digital model — three-dimensional copy of the lighthouse, observable and computer-tested.
currencies — pieces engraved from the lighthouse for almost a century in Alexandria.
Ostia — port of Rome where Emperor Claude built a tower inspired by Alexandria's lighthouse.
Alexandria lighthouse — a huge tower that guided the ships near Alexandria, one of the seven wonders of the ancient world.
Pharos — island where the lighthouse was located, in front of the port of Alexandria.
Pline the Old — Roman writer who said the lighthouse cost eight hundred talents.
Ptolemy First — King who started the lighthouse construction around 297 B.C.
talent — mass unit of approximately 21 kg, used to measure the value of the lighthouse.
earthquake — seismic event that dropped the lighthouse and part of Alexandria.
remains — stone blocks at the bottom of the water, witnesses to the lost lighthouse.
