Consign. Choose the right answer, then explain your choice based on what the video says.

1. How many grams of cooking salt can dissolve in a litre of 20 degrees water?

A) 180 g

B) 360 g

C) 2000 g

D) 50 g

Explain why you chose this answer

See a possible answer
Good answer: B) 360 g. In water at twenty degrees, the kitchen salt has a solubility of about three hundred and sixty grams per litre.

2. What is the point of knowing solubility in everyday life?

A) To choose a dessert

B) To measure the volume of a liquid

C) To make a fire

D) To prepare solutions whose amount of solute must be accurate

Explain why you chose this answer

See a possible answer
Good answer: D) To prepare solutions whose amount of solute must be accurate. A pharmacist prepares solutions whose amount of solute must be accurate.

3. What is the term for the substance that dissolves in a liquid?

A) A) The solvent

B) C) The Solute

C) D) The solution

D) B) The homogeneous mixture

Explain why you chose this answer

See a possible answer
Good answer: B) C) Solute. The dissolved substance is called the solute.
