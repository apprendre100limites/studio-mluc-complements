Glossary

Mixtures

dissolution — The process by which a solute is dispersed into a solvent.
heterogeneous — A mixture where one sees several different phases or layers.
homogeneous — A mixture where only one liquid is seen with the naked eye.
limit — The point where the solute can no longer dissolve further in the solvent.
Miscibles — Liquids that mix into a single homogeneous liquid.
non-miscible — Liquids that remain separated into two separate layers.
phase — Each layer visible in a mixture, such as water and oil.
saturated — A solution that has reached its solubility limit and can no longer dissolve further.
solubility — The maximum solute mass that a litre of solvent can dissolve.
solute — The substance that dissolves in the liquid, like sugar in the water.
solution — The homogeneous mixture obtained when the solute dissolves in the solvent.
solvent — The liquid that dissolves the solute, for example water.
