Mixtures

  • Solute is the substance that dissolves, solvent is the liquid that dissolves it.
  • The mixture of solute and solvent forms a homogeneous solution.
  • Solubility indicates the maximum solute mass that can be dissolved in a litre of solvent.
  • When solubility is reached, the solution is saturated and the surplus remains at the bottom.
  • Two liquids are miscible if they form a single homogeneous liquid, otherwise they are not miscible.
  • The oil does not mix with water because it is less dense, creating two layers.
  • A mixture is recognized by observing the number of visible phases.

What's the point?

The pharmacist prepares solutions with a precise amount of solute, the painter chooses the solvent adapted to his type of paint, and the aquaculturer controls the concentration of salt in the water of the basins. These concepts are therefore useful in health, construction, agriculture and research.

If only one sentence is retained
