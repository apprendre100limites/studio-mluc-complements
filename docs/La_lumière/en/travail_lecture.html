Light

Consign. Choose the level of reading that suits you. Read the text, then note the essentials in the right column.

Level 1 · Simplified reading

Without light, you can't see anything. The light comes from a primary source such as the Sun or a lamp. The moon does not create light, it reflects it. An object that receives light diffuses it. Part of this light enters the eye and you see the object.

The light moves in a straight line. We represent it by a ray with an arrow. In the air, the rays remain straight. The Sun takes about eight minutes to arrive. The moon sends light in about a third in a second.

An opaque object blocks the light and creates a shadow. The closer the lamp is, the bigger the shadow. The longer the lamp is, the smaller the shadow. A very strong light can hurt the eye. The Sun, a class three or four laser, and the arc of a weld are dangerous. We wear special glasses to watch an eclipse.

Level 2 · Standard reading

Hey! Without light, nothing is visible. The light comes from a primary source such as the Sun, a lit lamp, a candle flame or a lit screen. The Moon does not create light; It receives that of the Sun and returns it in all directions, it is said that it diffuses light. When a book is lit, it diffuses light and a part enters your eye, allowing you to see it.

In the air, the light moves straight. It is represented by a radius, a straight line with an arrow. The sun through the mist shows these straight rays. A beam is a set of rays from the same source, limited by two extreme rays. Light travels very quickly, about three hundred thousand kilometers per second. It takes a little over eight minutes to come from the Sun and about a second a third from the Moon.

When an opaque object blocks light, it creates a lightless area: shadow. If a small lamp is placed near the object, the rays that near the edges spread and the shadow becomes large. By moving away from the lamp, the boundary rays become almost parallel and the shadow narrows, approaching the size of the object.

Some lights are dangerous. Three cases can cause irreversible damage: the Sun, a class three or four laser, and the electric arc of a welding. To observe an eclipse, it takes special glasses, ordinary sunglasses are not enough.

Level 3 · in-depth reading

Hey! Without light, nothing is visible. The light comes from a primary source such as the Sun, a lit lamp or the flame of a candle. The Moon does not create light; It receives solar light and returns it in all directions, making it a diffuse object. Thus, when a book is lit, it spreads light and part of it enters your eye, allowing you to see it.

In the air, the light moves straight. It is represented by a radius, a straight line with an arrow. A beam of light is simply a group of rays that originate from the same source and are bounded by two boundary rays. The speed of light is about three hundred thousand kilometers per second; It takes a little over eight minutes to come from the Sun and about a second a third from the Moon.

When the light encounters an opaque object, it does not cross it. Behind this object is an area without light, i.e. a shadow. If you place a small lamp near an object, the rays that near the edges spread a lot and the shadow becomes big. By moving away from the lamp, the limit rays become almost parallel, the shadow shrinks and approaches the size of the object. The model of the radius thus allows to predict the shape and size of a shadow with a simple diagram.

Overly intense light can damage the eye permanently, either by direct observation or after reflection. Three cases are mentioned: the Sun, a class three or four laser, and the electric arc of a welding. To observe an eclipse, it takes specially designed glasses, ordinary sunglasses are not enough.

Key vocabulary

  • light : Visible energy emission that allows to see objects.
  • shadow : Lightless area behind an opaque object.
  • radius : Straight line that represents the path of light.
  • broadcasting : Object that returns the received light in all directions.
  • opaque : Object that does not let light pass.
