Light

Hey! Without light, you see nothing.

SEE A PURPOSE

A primary source produces the light it emits: the Sun, a lit lamp, a candle flame, a lit screen.

The Moon, it does not produce light. It receives the Sun and sends it back in all directions: it is said to spread it. It's a diffuse object.

How do you see a book? A source enlightens him, the book spreads this light, and a part enters your eye. Without light coming into your eye, you see nothing.

To be remembered. Without light coming into your eye, you see nothing.
Be careful. Think the moon produces its own light.

THE TRAJECTORY

In the air, light spreads in a straight line. It is represented by a ray of light: a straight line with an arrow.

You see it when the sun crosses the mist: the rays are straight. A beam of light is a set of rays from the same source; It is represented by its two limit rays.

The light goes very fast: about three hundred thousand kilometers per second. It takes a little more than eight minutes to come from the Sun, and about a second a third from the Moon.

To be remembered. In the air, light spreads in a straight line.
Be careful. Believing that the light naturally curves in the air.

THE BODIES

The light does not pass through an opaque object. Behind him there is a lightless area: it is the shadow.

Place a small lamp near an object: the rays around its edges are wide apart, and the shadow on the screen is large.

Keeps the lamp away: the limit rays become almost parallel, and the shadow narrows, until it approaches the size of the object.

The light beam model thus predicts the size of a shadow with a simple diagram.

To be remembered. The light does not pass through an opaque object.
Be careful. Think that the color of the object influences the size of the shadow.

DANGERS

Overly intense light can cause permanent damage to the eye, either directly or after reflection. Three cases: the Sun, a class three or four laser, and the electric arc of a solder.

To observe an eclipse, you need special glasses for that. Ordinary sunglasses are not enough.

To be remembered. Too much light can cause permanent damage to the eye.
Be careful. Use regular sunglasses to watch an eclipse.

What's the point?

An architect predicts the shadow of a building on its neighbours. An optician makes glasses following the path of light rays. An astronomer studies the light coming from the stars.

Conclusion

To sum up: one sees an object if light coming from him enters the eye.
