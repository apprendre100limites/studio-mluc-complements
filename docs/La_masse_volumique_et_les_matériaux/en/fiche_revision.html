Density and materials

  • Density is mass divided by volume.
  • It is expressed in kg/m³ or g/cm³.
  • Each material has its own density.
  • The density makes it possible to recognize a material and predict the buoyancy.
  • An alloy is a mixture of metal with other elements.
  • A composite material combines at least two immiscible materials.

Figures and formulas

  • 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³
  • wood density: < 1 g/cm³
  • iron density: 7.9 g/cm³
