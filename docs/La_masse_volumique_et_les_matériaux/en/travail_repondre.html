Density and materials

Consign. Choose the right answer, then explain your choice based on what the video says.

1. What is the density to recognize?

A) D) Hardness of a material

B) C) A material from its mass and volume

C) B) The temperature of a material

D) A) The color of a material

Explain why you chose this answer

See a possible answer
Good answer: B) C) A material from its mass and volume. Each material has its own density: it is a characteristic property.

2. What is the example given to calculate the density of an object?

A) C) An iron cube of 2 cm side with a mass of 15 g

B) A) A wooden cube of 2 cm side with a mass of 10 g

C) B) A 2 cm side aluminum cube with a mass of 21.6 g

D) D) A copper cube 2 cm side with a mass of 18 g

Explain why you chose this answer

See a possible answer
Good answer: C) B) A 2 cm side aluminum cube with a mass of 21.6 g. A two-centimetre side aluminum cube has a volume of eight cubic centimeters and a mass of twenty-one decimal six grams. Its density is twenty-one decimal six divided by eight, i.e. two decimal seven grams per cubic centimetre.
