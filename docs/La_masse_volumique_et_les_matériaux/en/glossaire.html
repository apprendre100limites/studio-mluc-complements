Glossary

Density and materials

alloy — a mixture of a metal with other elements to obtain specific properties
reinforced concrete — a material formed of concrete and steel bars
insertion — the insertion of smaller atoms between atoms of a metal
density — the mass divided by the volume, expressed in kg/m³ or g/cm³
property — a characteristic of a material, such as its strength or lightness
substitution — the replacement of atoms of one metal by atoms of another element
