Sound

We hear sounds all day, but what is a sound? In space, no one hears a shout because there is nothing to carry sound.

The nature of sound

A sound is a vibration: a very rapid back-and-forth motion of matter particles.

A guitar string, your vocal cords, a speaker diaphragm: all vibrate. An object that vibrates and produces sound is called a sound source. You can feel it yourself by placing two fingers on your throat and saying “aaaah”.

Key points. A sound results from the vibration of an object, called a sound source.

Propagation of sound

The vibration is transmitted from one particle to the next: one particle sets its neighbor in motion, which sets another in motion. What travels is the vibration, not the matter.

Without particles, no sound. In a vacuum, sound does not propagate. A material medium is required: air, water, or a solid.

Sound travels faster in water than in air, and even faster in steel. In air at twenty degrees, it moves at about three hundred forty meters per second. Example: lightning from a storm is seen, then five seconds later thunder is heard, the sound having traveled about one thousand seven hundred meters.

Key points. Sound propagates only in a material medium, by transmitting vibration from particle to particle.
Attention. Thinking that sound propagates in a vacuum.

Listen and measure

The ear receives the vibrations and turns them into signals that the brain understands. A microphone does the same thing, but produces electrical variations.

The sound level is measured in decibels with a sonometer: whispering, 60 dB conversation, 80 dB dense circulation, 100-110 dB concert. Beyond about 85 dB, prolonged exposure can permanently damage the hearing.

To be remembered. Sound is measured in decibels, and levels above 85 dB can damage hearing.

Protecting his hearing

To listen to safe music, you need to get away from the speakers, take regular breaks, limit the volume of the helmet and, in concert, wear plugs.

A full helmet can be comfortable, but the damage caused by the noise is permanent.

To be remembered. The volume and duration of exposure to sound must be limited to protect the ears.

What's the point?

These concepts make it possible to equip people who are deaf or hard of hearing, to record and regulate the volume of concerts, to treat hearing and to use ultrasound to see inside the body.

They are useful in health, music, cinema, safety at work and boat sonars.

To be remembered. Sound knowledge is applied in health, music, cinema, safety and navigation.

What's the point?

This knowledge helps to protect our audition and to use sound in many areas. They are useful in health, music, cinema, safety at work and boat sonars. They are employed by audioprosthetists, sound engineers, ENT doctors and ultrasound specialists.

Conclusion

In summary, sound is a vibration that only propagates in a material medium, is measured in decibels and requires protection beyond 85 dB.
