Glossary

Pharos Case 2/4: The Robot Portrait

archaeologists — Scientists who study the remains found in the water or underground to reconstruct the lighthouse's past.
graduated rope — A string with measurements used by a scientist to accurately measure every nook and cranny of the lighthouse.
cornice — A large protruding ledge located on the front of the headlight above the windows.
3D modeling — The method for drawing a building in three dimensions with a precise scale before constructing it.
alexandria lighthouse — The great ancient monument of Egypt, whose form researchers reconstruct with various clues.
coin — Antique coin engraved on the back with a simplified image of the lighthouse to serve as a visual witness.
portrait-robot — Detailed reconstruction of the appearance of the lighthouse from the descriptions of the various witnesses.
block puzzle — All the heavy stones found underwater that archaeologists must assemble to rebuild the lighthouse.
step ramp — A large slope with steps supported by arches to climb to the lighthouse.
Taposiris Magna — A site west of Alexandria containing a small miniature lighthouse that reproduces the structure of the large lighthouse.
witnesses — People, objects or ancient stories that have seen the lighthouse and tell us about its past appearance.
tritons — Mythical marine creatures depicted as statues blowing into a conch at the corners of the lighthouse.
