Characteristics: They may be geographers, coins or travellers; their reliability depends on whether they have seen, measured or copied.
Examples: Strabon, the Greek geographer who visited Egypt; Ibn al-Shaykh, the scientist who measured the lighthouse floor by floor.
Non-examples: An author who writes tales of wonders without ever having seen the site; a modern tourist without historical knowledge.
