Pharos 4/4 case: The crime scene

  • On August 8, 1303, a large earthquake dropped Alexandria's lighthouse and destroyed about a third of the city.
  • Historical accounts do not agree on the extent of the damage or on any repairs to the lighthouse.
  • The researchers made a digital twin of the lighthouse from digitized blocks to simulate loads and test hypotheses.
  • Models of two towers (one right and one floor) are tested under load, shaking and tilt to compare their resistance.
  • These experiments show where a structure can yield and explain the collapse of the lighthouse.
  • Digital simulations help engineers anticipate weaknesses before building a bridge, building or dam.
  • The trades concerned are structural computing engineer, civil engineering engineer, architect and digital simulation specialist.
