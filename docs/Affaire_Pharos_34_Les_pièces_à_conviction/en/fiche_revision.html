Pharos case 3/4: Exhibits

  • Alexandria's lighthouse has been under water because of the subsidence of the coast since ancient times.
  • The engulfed site covers about one and a half hectares, two football fields, with blocks weighing up to forty tons.
  • Since 2012, photogrammetry has created 3D digital models from hundreds of covering photos.
  • As of 1 January 2020, 65 per cent of the site was digitized and 3,040 blocks were recorded in the database.
  • The method requires 20-30 photos of an immobile object, with soft light and plain background, to obtain a digital double.
  • 3D models measure, compare and study blocks without removing them from the water.
  • In June 2025, some 20 large blocks were reassembled for study and digitization.

What's the point?

It is used to draw maps and plans, preserve heritage, create scenery for video games and movies, and control buildings without going up there. The professions that use it are archaeologist diver, topographer, geometer and three-dimensional digitization specialist.

If only one sentence is retained

Photogrammetry transforms photos into 3D models to study underwater remains without moving them.
