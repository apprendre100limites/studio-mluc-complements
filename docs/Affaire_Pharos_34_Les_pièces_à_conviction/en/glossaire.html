Glossary

Pharos case 3/4: Exhibits

stone blocks — Huge pieces of stone, fragments of columns and statues found on the bottom
terms of reference — A list of mission specifications, such as number of photos and constraints
Double digital — A complete numerical representation of an object, which can be rotated and measured
investigator — The researcher conducting the study of the underwater site to collect data
height — The object must be photographed under several heights to allow the construction of the model
three-dimensional model — A complete digital representation that can be rotated, measured and moved virtually
digitized — The site or objects have been transformed into digital data for analysis
lighthouse — The main object of the engulfed site, a construction that stood at the eastern tip of the peninsula of Pharos
photogrammetry — A method that uses many photos to create a three-dimensional model of an object
diver — Diver conducts traditional underwater site surveys
reflections — Objects must have relief and no reflections for photos
Subsidence — The phenomenon that caused the site to dive under the Mediterranean since ancient times
