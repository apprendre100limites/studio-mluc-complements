Glossary

Sinkholes in Turkey: when limestone disappears beneath our feet

carbonic acid — acid formed by water and carbon dioxide.
Limestone — rock containing calcium carbonate.
doline — closed, circular depression formed by the dissolution of limestone.
collapse — sudden subsidence of the ground due to the dissolution of limestone.
irrigation — method of supplying water to plants for their cultivation.
Karst — landscape formed by the dissolution of limestone.
karstic — relating to a landscape formed by the dissolution of limestone.
Groundwater — Body of groundwater that feeds springs and water tables.
Stalactite — drop of limestone hanging from the ceiling of caves.
vinegar — liquid containing ethanoic acid.
