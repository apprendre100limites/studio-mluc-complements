Experience sheet: Limestone and vinegar

Video: Dolines in Turkey: when limestone disappears under our feet

Equipment

A piece of limestone (or chalk, or a shell), a patty of quartz or granite, white vinegar, two cups or transparent glasses, protective glasses.

Protocol

Place the limestone in the first cup and the cake in the second. Pour the same amount of vinegar on each. Watch for a minute.

Due comment

Bubbles appear on limestone, not on the scab.

Interpretation

The gas released is carbon dioxide. Only the rock that contains calcium carbonate reacts. To verify the nature of the gas, it can be barboted in lime water, which is disturbed (if the material is available).

Equation

CaCO3 + 2 CH3COOH → Ca(CH3COO)2 + H2O + CO2

Model limit

Vinegar is much more acidic than rain. The reaction is seen in seconds, while in the natural state it takes thousands of years.

Security

Vinegar is safe, but it is avoided in the eyes.
