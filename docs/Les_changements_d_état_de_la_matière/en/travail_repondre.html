Changes in the state of matter

Consign. Choose the right answer, then explain your choice based on what the video says.

1. What concept does the video associate with stable temperature during a state change of a pure body?

A) Boil

B) Air pressure

C) Evaporation

D) The temperature range

Explain why you chose this answer

See a possible answer
Good answer: D) The temperature range. This temperature that remains stable during the state change is called a temperature level.

2. What happens to the salt water temperature during boiling, according to the video?

A) She continues to climb

B) It stops at 0°C

C) It is gradually falling

D) It remains stable at 100 °C

Explain why you chose this answer

See a possible answer
Good answer: A) It continues to climb. With a mixture, like salt water, there is no bearing: the temperature continues to rise during boiling.

3. How does a refrigerator use changes in condition to produce cold?

A) Compressing the inside ice

B) By evaporating a coolant

C) By increasing air temperature

D) By burning methane gas

Explain why you chose this answer

See a possible answer
Good answer: B) By evaporating a coolant. The cold of your fridge or air conditioning comes from changes in condition: a liquid that evaporates cools, and a compressed gas liquefies.
