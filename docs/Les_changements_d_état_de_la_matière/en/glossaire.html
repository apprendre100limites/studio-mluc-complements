Glossary

Changes in the state of matter

altitude — Height above sea level where the air is thinner.
pressure cooker — Utensil with high pressure increasing the boiling point.
pure body — Substance with constant state change temperature.
boiling — Formation of vapour bubbles throughout the liquid.
evaporation — Slow change at the surface of the liquid at any temperature.
merger — Switching from solid to liquid, called melting.
liquefaction — Changing from gas to liquid, called liquefaction.
mixture — Mixing without temperature plateau during boiling.
temperature plateau — Stable temperature during a change of state.
atmospheric pressure — Air pressure on us, measured by a barometer.
solidification — Change from liquid to solid, called solidification.
spraying — Change from liquid to gas, called vaporization.
