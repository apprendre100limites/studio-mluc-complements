Volume and mass: measuring matter

In this video, you learn to measure a volume, weigh a liquid and understand the relationship between mass and volume.

Measuring volume

Volume is the place that matter takes. The volume of a liquid is measured with a graduated test piece.

For a rock, the volume is recorded by subtracting the volume of water before and after the rock is immersed.

Volume is measured in cubic metres, litres and millilitres in the international system.

To be remembered. The volume of a liquid is measured using a graduated test piece.

The state of matter

The material exists in three states: solid, liquid and gas.

A solid has a clean shape, a liquid takes the form of its container and a gas occupies all the space.

These states determine the behaviour of matter.

To be remembered. A liquid takes the form of its container, while a solid keeps its shape clean.

Mass and water

To weigh a liquid, you use a balance and press "tare" to reset the display to zero.

A litre of water has a mass of one kilogram, so a millilitre of water weighs one gram.

However, other liquids such as oil have a different mass for the same volume.

To be remembered. A litre of water has a mass of one kilogram.
Be careful. Be careful, the oil does not weigh a kilogram for a litre. It weighs about nine hundred and twenty grams.

Connect mass and volume

The mass of a substance is proportional to its volume.

For water, one hundred millilitres weigh one hundred grams and two hundred and fifty millilitres, two hundred and fifty grams.

For oil, one hundred millilitres weigh about ninety-two grams and two hundred and fifty millilitres, about two hundred and thirty grams.

This report is called density.

To be remembered. The mass is proportional to the volume of the same substance.

What's the point?

These concepts are useful in understanding how to measure and weigh correctly, which is important in many trades such as logistics, pharmacy and cooking. In everyday life, these concepts can be used to fill water glasses or weigh ingredients.

Conclusion

We learned to measure volume, weigh a liquid, and understand the relationship between mass and volume.
