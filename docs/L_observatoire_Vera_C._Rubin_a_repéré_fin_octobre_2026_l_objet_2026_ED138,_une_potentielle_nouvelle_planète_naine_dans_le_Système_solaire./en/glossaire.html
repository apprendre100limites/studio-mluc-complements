Glossary

At the end of October 2026, the Vera C. Rubin Observatory spotted object 2026 ED138, a potential new dwarf planet in the Solar System.

star — A celestial body that revolves around the Sun, like a planet.
candidate — An object which could become a dwarf planet if its measures confirm the criteria.
gravity — Force that attracts matter to the center of an object and makes it spherical when it is large enough.
Vera C. Rubin Observatory — Observatory in Chile whose telescope photographes the sky night after night.
orbit — The path an object follows around the Sun.
dwarf planet — A star that revolves around the Sun, is massive enough to be round and has not cleansed its orbit.
Pluto — A famous example of a dwarf planet recognized since 2006.
sphere — Round shape of a sufficiently massive object under the effect of its gravity.
James Webb Space Telescope — Instrument used to observe in the infrared to accurately measure the size of distant objects.
astronomical unit — Average distance between Earth and Sun.
