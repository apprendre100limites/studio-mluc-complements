Introduce the concept of energy

  • Energy is the ability to do work or produce heat.
  • A pendulum has mechanical energy that transforms between potential energy and kinetics and then dissipates by friction.
  • The human body gets its energy from food and spends it to move, reflect and maintain its temperature.
  • Machines do not create energy, they convert it from one form to another, such as a motor or solar panel.
  • Energy sources can be renewable (sun, wind, water) or non-renewable (oil, coal, gas).
  • An energy chain describes the transfer and conversion stages of energy, from food to light of a lamp.
  • Energy never disappears, it only changes shape.

What's the point?

A renewable energy engineer designs solar or wind farms, a dietician adjusts power to energy needs, and an electrician installs systems that waste less energy. This knowledge is useful in the fields of energy, health, building and sport.
