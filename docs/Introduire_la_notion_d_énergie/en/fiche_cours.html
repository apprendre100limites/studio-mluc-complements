Introduce the concept of energy

We often talk about solar energy, food energy or energy savings, but what is energy really? We will understand why the energy of a swing that stops does not disappear.

Introduction

The text begins by greeting the spectator and reminds us that we often hear about solar energy, food energy and energy savings.

He asks the question "what is energy?" by giving the example of a swing that stops on its own, whose energy has not evaporated.

To be remembered. Energy never disappears, it transforms.

Energy and Pendulum

A pendulum has energy when it swings, either because it is moving (kinetic energy) or because it is high (potential energy).

At the top of the race, the pendulum has its maximum altitude and its speed is zero; At the bottom, it has its minimum altitude and maximum speed.

The pendulum eventually stops: its energy has not disappeared, it has been transferred to the air by friction, warming it slightly.

To be remembered. The pendulum energy moves from potential energy to kinetic energy and transfers to air by friction.
Be careful. Do not believe that the pendulum energy disappears when it stops.

Body energy

The body uses the energy contained in food (intake) to cover the needs of movement, reflection and maintenance of temperature.

For an adult, the needs are of the order of eight thousand three hundred and sixty-eight to ten thousand four hundred and sixty kilojoules a day; in adolescence they are often higher if you are active.

Adapted power supplies, over time, as much energy as the body spends; too little leads to fatigue, too much leads to storage.

To be remembered. The body must receive as much energy as it spends to avoid fatigue or storage.
Be careful. Do not confuse energy intake with needs by eating without taking into account activity.

Machinery

A machine does not create energy, it converts it from a source: an engine transforms the energy of fuel, a solar panel that of light.

Some sources are renewable (sun, wind, water) as they renew as quickly as they are used, others are non-renewable (oil, coal, gas) because they are in limited stock.

In New Caledonia, much of the electricity still comes from fossil fuels such as coal and fuel oil, but solar and wind energy are growing.

To be remembered. A machine transforms the energy of a source without creating it.
Be careful. Do not think that a machine can create energy from nothing.

Energy chains

With a bike equipped with a dynamo, the chemical energy of food becomes energy of movement in the muscles, the dynamo converts it into electric energy, then the lamp turns it into light with a little lost heat.

These conversions are represented by a scheme called the energy chain, which shows the transfer and conversion of energy without destruction.

At each conversion, a small part of the energy turns into heat, but the total energy remains preserved.

To be remembered. An energy chain shows how energy transfers and converts from one form to another.
Be careful. Remember that each conversion results in a small loss in the form of heat.

What's the point?

These concepts are not only used for successful control, they are essential in several areas.

A renewable energy engineer designs solar and wind farms, a dietician adapts the power supply to a person's needs, and an electrician installs equipment that wastes less energy.

This knowledge is useful in the energy, health, building and sport sectors.

To be remembered. These concepts are useful in the fields of energy, health, construction and sport.
Be careful. Do not believe that these notions are only used to pass control.

What's the point?

Understanding energy allows us to manage our daily needs, optimize devices and select appropriate sources. In everyday life it is applied in power supply, heating, transport and use of electrical appliances. Trades such as renewable energy engineer, dietician or electrician use this knowledge.

Conclusion

In short, energy transfers and converts without disappearing, the body has needs and inputs, sources are renewable or not, and conversions are described by an energy chain.
