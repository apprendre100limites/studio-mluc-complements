Trajectory

Name

Definition

All the successive positions that an object occupies during its movement.

Characteristics

Examples

Non-examples

See a possible answer
Characteristics: It describes the route of an object; it may be straight or circular; it shows how the object moves.
Examples: Lighthouses of a car on a right road; the basket of a large wheel.
Non-examples: A frozen point without displacement; a senseless random trajectory.
Speed

Name

Definition

The speed with which an object moves.

Characteristics

Examples

Non-examples

See a possible answer
Characteristics: It measures how much distance is travelled; it varies according to movement; it can be medium or instantaneous.
Examples: The meter of a car displaying ninety; Usain Bolt running 100 meters.
Non-examples: An object still at rest; A braked car.
Instant speed

Name

Definition

The speed that a meter indicates at a precise moment of the journey.

Characteristics

Examples

Non-examples

See a possible answer
Characteristics: It concerns a specific moment of the journey; the meter gives it alone; It differs from the average speed.
Examples: The number displayed on the counter at the moment you look; The speed of Usain Bolt at a precise moment.
Non-examples: The average speed on the entire journey; the speed of a motionless object.
Transfer

Name

Definition

A change in direction of movement.

Characteristics

Examples

Non-examples

See a possible answer
Characteristics: In a turn, the direction changes; the wheels change the direction of speed; The tighter it is, the slower it is.
Examples: A car turning left; a big wheel that makes a complete tour.
Non-examples: A straight movement without changing direction; a car that brakes without turning.
Distance

Name

Definition

The length travelled by an object.

Characteristics

Examples

Non-examples

Characteristics: It is used to calculate speed; it is measured in kilometres or metres; It varies according to the route.
Examples: One hundred and fifty kilometres travelled in two hours; 30 kilometers driven by a cyclist.
Non-examples: The distance remaining at the starting point without moving forward; the distance of a motionless object.
