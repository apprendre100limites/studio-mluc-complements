Introduction to speed and movement

  • The trajectory is the set of successive positions of a moving object.
  • A rectilinear motion has aligned positions, while a circular motion forms a circle or arc.
  • The average speed is calculated by dividing the distance travelled by the duration of the journey.
  • The instantaneous speed corresponds to the value indicated by the meter at a specific time.
  • Speed has three characteristics: a value, a direction and a direction.
  • In a turn, the direction of the speed changes and you have to slow down before entering.
  • To go from km/h to m/s, divide by 3.6; for the reverse, multiply by 3.6.

Figures and formulas

  • 150 km ÷ 2 h = 75 km/h
  • 100 m ÷ 9.58 s ≈ 10.4 m/s ≈ 38 km/h
  • 1 km/h = 0.277... m/s (division by 3.6)
  • 30 km ÷ 1.5 h = 20 km/h

What's the point?

Road engineers design turns and set speed limits, and radars measure the speed of vehicles to enhance road safety. Athletic coaches analyze the speed of athletes in order to optimize their performance.

If we only remember one sentence
