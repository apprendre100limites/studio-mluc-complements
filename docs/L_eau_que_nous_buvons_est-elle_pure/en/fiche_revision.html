Is the water we drink pure?

  • Tap water is a mixture.
  • Anhydrous copper sulphate proves the presence of water.
  • Evaporation reveals dissolved salts.
  • Lime water reveals carbon dioxide.
  • Distilled water is almost pure.
  • The pH measures acidity.

What's the point?

It's used to check that tap water is good for drinking and to locate what's dissolved. They are needed by technicians, labs and hydrologists who monitor water everywhere.
