Glossary

Is the water we drink pure?

Carbon dioxide — a colourless and odourless gas, also known as carbon dioxide
pure water — water containing only one substance, without impurities
evaporation — the transfer of water from liquid to vapour
mixture — water containing several substances
pH — a measurement of the acidity of a solution, ranging from 0 to 14
dry residue — what remains after all the water in a liquid has been evaporated
dissolved salts — substances which mix in water and do not see themselves
copper sulphate anhydrous — a white powder used to detect water
