Glossary

The microscopic scale of matter

compression — It is the approximation of the particles of a gas by reducing the space between them.
gaseous state — The particles are very far away, moving quickly and filling all the space.
liquid state — The particles stay tight but slide on each other.
solid state — The particles are tight, tidy and vibrate without changing places.
particulate model — It is the model that describes matter as small particles.
Brownian movement — It is the random movement of invisible particles that push visible grains.
pressure — It comes from the shock of gas particles against the walls of the container.
solution — Mixture of solvent and solute particles, such as sugar dissolved in water.
