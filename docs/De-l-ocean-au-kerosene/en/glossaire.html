Glossary

From the ocean to kerosene

completely closed energy loop — System where the ship produces its own fuel at sea, without external delivery.
molybdenum carbide — Durable material used for catalysts, protected against salt.
oceanic corrosion — Wear of equipment caused by ocean salt.
sea water — It is rich in dissolved gases, notably carbon dioxide and hydrogen.
large closed carbon cycle — System where carbon comes from and returns to the ocean, reducing dependence on fossil fuels.
aviation kerosene — Fuel extracted directly from the ocean.
ECEM module — Ultra‑high‑tech filter capturing gases with 92 % efficiency.
carbon monoxide — Form of transformed carbon dioxide, CO.
tanker ships — Ships that refuel at sea.
nuclear reactors — Energy source that allows aircraft carriers to sail for decades without refueling.
Fischer–Tropsch synthesis — Chemical process recombining extracted gases to produce kerosene.
